Privacy Policy Support

Who we are

Stillio is made by Bekir Sucikaran. This policy covers the iOS app and these public pages. For a privacy question, email bekirsucikaran00@gmail.com.

What the app does

Stillio uses the camera to watch for movement while the app is open and in the foreground. The preview and the motion check stay on the device. Motion is a change in brightness inside the area you select. The app does not identify people, faces, or objects, and it does not guarantee that every movement will be detected.

If you turn capture on, Stillio can save one photo, or a silent video of up to 10 seconds, in the app’s own storage on the device. The video is recorded without microphone audio. Settings such as sensitivity, alarm sound, vibration, capture mode, detection area, language, and whether you have finished the introduction are also stored on the device.

Permissions

  • Camera. Stillio asks for camera access so it can show the preview, watch the selected area, and record an optional photo or silent video. If you deny or restrict the camera, monitoring cannot run.
  • Photos, add only. Stillio asks to add photos only after you tap Save to Photos. It asks to add items, not to browse or read your photo library. If you deny that access, the copy is not added to Photos and the file already stored in Stillio stays there.
  • Microphone. Stillio does not request microphone access and does not record sound with a capture.
  • Tracking. Stillio does not request permission to track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Sharing and Save to Photos

Nothing is shared, and nothing is added to Photos, unless you tap the control yourself.

  • Share copies the photo or video so the system share sheet can hand it to an app or destination you choose. What that destination does with the copy is outside Stillio.
  • Save to Photos copies the photo or video into Photos. Stillio keeps its own copy.
  • Delete in Stillio removes that event’s files from the app. It does not remove a copy already saved to Photos, and it does not remove a copy you already shared.

Backups and sync

Stillio does not upload the preview, photos, or videos to a Stillio server. A copy you save to Photos can sync if iCloud Photos or another Photos service is on, following the settings on your Apple ID and devices. An iCloud or computer backup of the device can also include the app’s local files, following your backup settings. Those are Apple or destination services, not a transfer started by Stillio.

Accounts, advertising, analytics, and the network

Stillio does not create an account and does not ask you to sign in. The app does not show advertising and does not include an advertising or analytics SDK. It does not use a tracking identifier.

Monitoring, detection, capture, playback, alarms, and settings do not send the camera preview, photos, or videos anywhere. The Privacy Policy and Support links in the app open these pages in the browser only after you tap them. They are not contacted while Stillio is monitoring.

These web pages

This Privacy Policy and the Support page are static files hosted on GitHub Pages. The pages do not contain a form, do not set their own cookies, and do not load third-party scripts. Opening a page is a request to GitHub. GitHub may process ordinary request details, such as IP address, browser, and the address of the page, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Stillio does not operate that log, and these pages never receive the camera preview, photos, or videos from the app.
